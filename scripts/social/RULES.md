# LuxWeb social posts

Rules for anything posted to LuxWeb Studio's social accounts. Applies to every
draft written in this repo, to the drafts the weekly demo routine (luxweb-demos)
writes into `scripts/social/queue.yaml`, and to `/social-batch`. Kevin approves
every post before it goes out. Never schedule unapproved copy, never rewrite
approved copy.

## Who's talking

Kevin Bandison, first person. "I'm Kevin" where it fits, not every post.
Cookout version of what he does: "I'm a web developer with an agency."
LuxWeb Studio is an **agency**. Never "shop", never "studio" as a common noun.

## Voice (taken from Kevin's real client messages)

- Plain words, complete sentences, correct punctuation. Capitalize normally.
- Explain the *why* in one sentence, then stop. Kevin explains, he does not
  over-explain. If a post needs a second sentence of justification, cut it.
- Honest to a fault. "I'm not going to lie," "I'm not a big sports guy," "I
  haven't calculated yet." Admitting a limit is part of the voice.
- Reassuring, not salesy. "You're good, don't worry about it." "Take your
  time." "I'll work with you either way."
- Parentheses for asides. Commas and periods, never em dashes.
- One exclamation point per post at most, usually zero. "lol" and the odd
  emoji are fine. Nothing stacked.
- Short. A post reads like a text message to someone he likes, not a caption
  written for an audience.
- Talks *to* one person, not *at* a market. "If you need a website" beats
  "business owners who need websites."

## The AI filter (run on every draft, fail on any hit)

Kevin's bar: if it's overused or cliché it makes him cringe. The lists below
are the known offenders; treat anything in their family the same way.

**Banned words:** elevate, unlock, unleash, seamless, seamlessly, effortless,
hassle-free, game-changer, game changing, next-level, cutting-edge,
state-of-the-art, top-notch, robust, leverage, empower, harness, streamline,
transform, boost, skyrocket, supercharge, delve, dive in, dive into, journey,
landscape, navigate, realm, tapestry, testament, foster, ensure, utilize,
curated, crafted, bespoke, tailored, stunning, vibrant, thriving, bustling,
nestled, passionate, dedicated, mission, solutions, synergy, innovative,
revolutionary, in order to, a plethora of, plethora.

**Banned phrases:** in today's fast-paced world, in today's digital age, look
no further, whether you're a ... or a ..., it's not just ..., it's ..., it's not
about ..., it's about ..., let's be honest, let's be real, at the end of the
day, here's the thing, the truth is, imagine a world, picture this, stand out
from the crowd, take your business to the next level, ready to ...?, don't
miss out, limited time only (unless it is a real sale), we're thrilled to
announce, we're excited to share, say hello to, introducing, officially on,
let's build something great together, I hope this helps, rest assured, that
being said, without further ado, it's worth noting, in the ever-changing.

**Banned structures:**
- Em dashes. Any. Use a comma, a period, or parentheses.
- Triplets: "fast, clean, and custom." Two things or one.
- Contrast-for-drama: "Not templates. Not page builders. Real code."
- A rhetorical question as the opener.
- One-word drama sentences. "Simple." "Done." "Period."
- Every paragraph ending on a punchline.
- Bullet lists inside a caption.
- The pointing emoji 👇 and "link below" theater.
- Sign-off slogans.
- Explaining what business owners "don't care about." Kevin's note: the
  templates/page-builders pitch is a developer's selling point, not an
  owner's. Say "custom" once if it matters and move on.

## Content types (rotate; three posts a week)

1. **Demo site reveal.** Kevin builds one or two a week. Screenshot template,
   headline like "Built this one in two days." Say what kind of business it's
   for and one thing it does well. Later: short scroll videos for TikTok/Reels.
2. **Local sites, roasted gently.** Never name a real business. The common
   sins: not updated in years, phone number buried, hours wrong, broken on a
   phone, design from another decade.
3. **How a project actually goes.** Only with confirmed current facts (see
   above). This is the trust-builder; nobody else posts it.
4. **Stories from real jobs.** Anonymized. Kevin signs off on each one.
5. **The free homepage design offer.** About once a month, said plainly.
6. **Kevin.** Veteran, Black owned, self-taught, why he started. Every few
   weeks, not every week.

A typical week: one demo, one tip or roast, one from types 3 to 6.

## Per-network adaptation (one approved post, five versions)

- **Facebook:** the base copy. No hashtags. Full URL is fine.
- **Instagram:** base copy, URL becomes "link in bio," then four or five
  hashtags on their own line at the end. Image required.
- **X:** cut to under 280 characters. Keep the one fact that matters. No
  hashtags. URL at the end.
- **LinkedIn:** base copy, a touch more context about the business side, no
  slang, three hashtags at the end. Posts from the LuxWeb company page.
- **Google Business:** two or three sentences, no hashtags, no "link in bio,"
  plus a Learn more button to the site. Think "what shows up when someone
  Googles LuxWeb."
